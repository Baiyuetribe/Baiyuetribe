About Mona

Mona, the GitHub Octocat, is more than just a mascot – she's a symbol of collaboration, innovation, and the open-source spirit that drives the global developer community. Created in 2008, this charming hybrid of a cat and an octopus has become one of the most recognizable icons in the tech world.

The Octocat was originally designed by Simon Oxley and later refined by GitHub's team. Her unique design represents the fusion of feline curiosity and the octopus's ability to multitask – perfect metaphors for the modern developer's workflow. With her friendly demeanor and adventurous spirit, Mona embodies the collaborative nature of software development.

Today, Mona appears in countless variations, each telling a different story and representing various aspects of the GitHub community. From astronaut Mona exploring the cosmos of code to ninja Mona stealthily fixing bugs, each iteration brings joy and personality to the platform that millions of developers call home.

Fun Facts About Mona

🎨

Original Designer

Mona was originally designed by Simon Oxley, the same artist who created the Twitter bird logo. The design was later purchased and refined by GitHub.

🌟

Born in 2008

The Octocat made her debut in 2008, the same year GitHub was founded. She's been the company's mascot from the very beginning.

🎭

Over 200 Variations

The GitHub Octodex features over 200 different variations of Mona, each created for special occasions, holidays, or community events.

🐙

Cat + Octopus = Octocat

The name "Octocat" comes from combining "octopus" and "cat," representing the multitasking abilities of developers and their curious nature.

🎯

Open Source Spirit

Many Octocat designs are contributed by the community, embodying the open-source philosophy that GitHub promotes.

🚀

Space Explorer

One of the most popular Octocat variations is the Astronaut Octocat, symbolizing exploration and reaching for the stars in coding.
